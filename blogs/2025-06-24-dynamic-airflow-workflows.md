Singularity Energy develops software solutions for utilities and grid operators to help entities operating on the U.S. electric grid accurately measure their greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and facilitate decisions that make the grid cleaner. As part of customer onboarding, we ingest generation and load data along with metadata that helps us calculate emissions and monitor load profiles.
